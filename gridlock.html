About the Show

The grid, from the people who run it.

GridLock takes the research questions Dr. Moses studies in the lab — power quality, grid resiliency, electromagnetic transients, renewable integration — and puts them in conversation with the engineers and operators solving them in the field. Each episode pairs technical depth with the practical reality of keeping power flowing.

Format

Long-form conversation

One host, one guest, a real problem from the field or the lab.

Audience

Engineers & students

Built for practicing engineers, utility teams, and students headed into the field.

Focus

Power in practice

Grid resiliency, power quality, renewables integration, and the failures that teach the most.

Your Host

Dr. Paul Moses

Dr. Moses is a tenured faculty member at the University of Oklahoma's School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, an Adjunct Professor at George Washington University, and the 2023 recipient of the National Science Foundation CAREER award. He brings two decades of research and hands-on engineering experience — from the Australian Collins Class Submarine program to smart grid resiliency research — to every conversation on the show.
